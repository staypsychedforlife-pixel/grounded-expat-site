You don’t have to know what’s wrong.

You just have to know what’s feeling hard.

Find the one below that sounds most like you at the moment. Each one points at a place to start, not a whole program. If two of them sound like you, that’s normal. Pick either.

The excitement has worn off

  • The Month-3 Dip · free
  • Rough Waters · for the homesickness, the doubt, and the exhaustion underneath
The hard middle

I miss home

You don’t necessarily want to go back. You miss the people, the ease, and the version of yourself who lived there.

  • The Goodbye Journal · free, for loving people from far away
  • How to deal with homesickness abroad
The hard middle

I’m lonely

You’re meeting people. You just haven’t found your people. You’re not cursed, it’s genuinely hard.

  • Making Friends in a New Country · free
  • Finding Your People Abroad · a step-by-step path from lonely to a life full of people
The hard middle

I’m exhausted

Not tired from one bad week. The deep, cumulative exhaustion of adapting all day, every day, in a place that doesn’t run on your defaults.

  • The Expat Burnout Workbook · name where your energy goes and rebuild a pace you can sustain
  • Expat burnout, and what actually helps

I don’t feel like myself

  • Rootless: Finding Home Within · belonging everywhere and nowhere

  • The Partner’s Journal · your grief, your invisible work, your becoming
  • The Expat Marriage Workbook · for facing it together
Life on the move

I’m living on the move

You can go anywhere. Some days that’s freedom and some days you’ve lost the thread of what it’s all for.

Going home

I’m going home

Going back isn’t going back. You’re not the person who left, and home didn’t stay still either.

  • The Repatriation Workbook · for coming home a different person than the one who left
  • Reverse culture shock
Not sure what you need
