Work with me · one to one

You made the move. That was the part you could plan for.

You’re functioning. Maybe doing pretty well. But somewhere along the way something got harder, and it isn’t the visa or the shipping. It’s home, or identity, or the friendships that haven’t quite happened, or not feeling like yourself here. That’s the part I work with.

Hi, I’m Stephanie

I’ve lived abroad long enough to know the hardest parts usually are the ones people don’t talk about. I’ve also worked in behavioral health since 2009 and I’m a licensed clinical social worker, which means I can go a bit further with you than “give it time.”

You might be here because

You’re doing fine, and you still don’t feel fine.

  • You love where you live and you’re lonelier than you expected to be.
  • You’re tired of explaining why something feels different now.
  • Your relationship has changed since the move and you can’t quite name how.
  • You don’t know whether you want to stay or go home.
  • Everyone assumes you’re having the adventure of a lifetime.
  • You miss the person you used to be more than you thought you would.
  • You’re back home and it doesn’t feel like home.
  • Nothing is really wrong. You just don’t feel like yourself.

That’s the territory I work in. Whether you’re getting ready to go, somewhere in the middle of it, or back home and finding that harder than you expected.

Worth saying plainly

A different kind of support.

I’m a licensed clinical social worker, and this isn’t therapy. No diagnosis, no treatment plan, and no assumption that something is wrong with you.

Sometimes you don’t need therapy. You need somewhere to think out loud, someone who understands the strange middle ground of living abroad, and enough perspective to work out what comes next.

Getting started

You don’t have to work this part out on your own.
