Why The Circle

There’s more to say than “It’s great.”

The people back home don’t quite understand. The people around you are still finding their way into your life. You can love where you are and still miss the people, ease, and version of yourself you left behind.

The Circle is a small, steady community for that honest middle. You can talk about what’s hard without first proving that you’re grateful, and without turning your life into a success story before it feels like one.

You can arrive with the thing you haven’t known how to say and start there.

Questions you can bring

You don’t need to have the right words.

These are examples of the kinds of questions you might bring to The Circle, not posts from members.

I chose this move. Why am I still so angry about everything I gave up?

How do I make friends when I’m tired of always being the one who reaches out?

What do I do with the guilt of missing home when my life here is good?

How do I know whether this is a hard season or a sign I want to leave?

Who it’s for

You might recognize yourself here.

You’re living someone else’s move

Their job, their dream, your whole life. You’re trying to find where you fit inside it.

The excitement has worn off

Daily life takes more out of you than it used to, and you’re wondering when this starts feeling easier.

Your life looks good from home

You’re grateful for it. You’re also lonely, homesick, or not quite yourself.

You’ve been away for years

You’ve built a life here and still don’t know where home is when someone asks.
