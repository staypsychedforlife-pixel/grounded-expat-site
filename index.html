Moving abroad changes more than your address.

You can love where you are and still miss home.

You can be grateful for the opportunity and still feel lonely. You can make friends and still feel like an outsider. You can know you made the right decision and still wonder, what have I done?

That’s the part of life abroad people don’t talk about much.

The Grounded Expat is for the inner side of living abroad: the homesickness, identity shifts, culture shock, loneliness, and quiet grief that can show up even when everything looks fine from the outside.

No therapy-speak. No pretending everything is an adventure. Just thoughtful, practical resources for figuring out who you are while you’re building a life somewhere new.

Who’s behind it

Hi, I’m Stephanie.

I’m a licensed clinical social worker, and I’ve spent most of my career working with people through trauma, grief, identity, shame, and major life transitions.

I’ve also spent the last few years living abroad myself.

And I’ve heard the same sentence in a hundred different forms:

I chose this. So what’s wrong with me for finding it hard?

Usually, nothing.

Moving abroad asks you to rebuild things that used to happen automatically: belonging, routine, identity, relationships, confidence, even your sense of home.

The Grounded Expat is where I put the tools and perspective I wish more people had while doing that work.
